Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the app, we store the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment information or any data beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service to you — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and filtering by date or category. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core features of the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data from our systems.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to run the app, but these providers do not have access to your expense data beyond what is necessary to operate the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided in the app.
